# Creative direction — Signal / Story / System

## Narrative

The site treats each project as an act of translation: raw material becomes a signal, the signal becomes an explanation, and the explanation becomes a tool. This joins analytics and AI to the user's application-development interests without presenting him as a senior practitioner.

## Visual system

- **Dark field:** obsidian, warm white and red establish the personal editorial layer.
- **Data accents:** mint is reserved for ocean/data visualizations, acid-lime for the agent workflow and warm sand for credit-risk material.
- **Type:** Anton for compressed display headlines, Caveat for brief handwritten annotations, IBM Plex Mono for system labels and Inter for body reading.
- **Composition:** cropped portrait, oversized wordmark, thin rules, asymmetric editorial layouts and custom vector project diagrams. Diagrams are representative visuals, never screenshots.
- **Technology section:** deliberate contrast. Warm grey canvas with repeating plus grid, solid black title block and nine restrained white tiles. It reads as an equipment / learning field and distinguishes tools used to build the portfolio from technologies still being explored.
- **Motion:** Motion handles local section and tile interactions. GSAP/ScrollTrigger owns scroll choreography. Their target properties do not overlap. R3F renders one optional low-density data field with an accessible, static CSS fallback.

## Section sequence

1. Cinematic portrait hero: personal identity + curiosity.
2. About: connect questions across data, AI and software.
3. Selected work: four distinct cases from four domains.
4. Technology grid: visual pause and transparent learning map.
5. Process: how investigation becomes communication.
6. Education, experience, publication and credentials.
7. Contact close.

## Responsive and inclusive behavior

Keep the hero's name/portrait composition at tablet; simplify the overlap at mobile while keeping the face unobscured. Use three/ two/one columns for the technology grid. 3D and nonessential motion are removed for reduced-motion preferences and narrow/low-capability contexts. Every tile remains focusable with its explanatory text available without hover.
